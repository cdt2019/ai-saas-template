The newsletter handles signup, confirmation, unsubscribe and plain-text campaigns. It requires Resend in production.

```bash
RESEND_API_KEY=...
EMAIL_FROM="YourBrand <hello@example.com>"
```

## Signup

Visitors subscribe from the site footer or the `/newsletter` page.

1. The visitor enters their email.
2. They receive a confirmation email.
3. The link opens a page where they press a button to confirm.

The button matters because mail scanners open links automatically. Requiring a press stops them from confirming an address on their own.

Signups are limited per IP by `LIMIT_NEWSLETTER_PER_IP_HOUR` (default 5). The response is the same whether or not the address is already subscribed, so the form can't be used to check who's on your list.

Unconfirmed signups are deleted after 30 days.

## Unsubscribe

Every campaign email supports two ways out:

- One-click unsubscribe through RFC 8058 `List-Unsubscribe` headers, which mail apps show as a built-in button
- An unsubscribe link in the email itself

## Managing subscribers

`/admin/newsletter` shows your subscribers. You can:

- Filter the list
- Export it as CSV
- Remove a subscriber

Treat the export as personal data, and store it accordingly.

## Sending a campaign

1. Open `/admin/newsletter` and write a plain-text campaign.
2. Choose the audience: all, English or Chinese subscribers.
3. Send a test to yourself and check it.
4. Send.

## Delivery

Campaigns are delivered in batches by the scheduled job `newsletter.send`, which runs every 10 minutes.

| Setting | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Batch size | `NEWSLETTER_BATCH_SIZE`, default 100 |
| Delay between emails | About 600 ms with a real provider |

If a send is interrupted, that email is marked failed and isn't retried. A failed email is better than the same person getting it twice.

### Plan for your volume

Your clock decides how fast a campaign goes out. At the default batch size:

| Trigger | Approximate throughput |
| --- | --- |
| Every 10 minutes | 600 emails per hour |
| Vercel Hobby (one tick a day) | 100 emails per day |

Check two limits before sending to a large list:

- **Function time.** 100 emails at 600 ms each take about 60 seconds, which is the Vercel Hobby limit. Lower the batch size on that plan.
- **Resend quota.** The free plan allows 3,000 emails a month and 100 a day.

See [Scheduled jobs](scheduled-jobs#triggers) to set up a more frequent trigger.
