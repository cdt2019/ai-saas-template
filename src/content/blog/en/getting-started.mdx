Welcome to Acme. This short guide walks you through your first run, how credits work, and where to find everything in your dashboard.

## 1. Create your account

Sign up with **Google**, **GitHub** or your email. New accounts get free credits right away, so you can try every tool before paying anything.

## 2. Run your first tool

Open **AI Studio** and pick a mode:

| Mode | What it does | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rewrite | Improves a paragraph you paste in | 1 credit |
| Image | Generates an image from a prompt | 5 credits |
| Video | Animates a photo you upload | 50 credits |

The cost is always shown before you start. If a run fails, its credits go straight back to your balance.

<Callout>
  Tip: on the home page you can type a prompt, press **Generate**, and it opens AI Studio with your prompt filled in.
</Callout>

## 3. Choose how to pay

- **Credit packs** are one-time purchases. Credits never expire.
- **Subscriptions** add fresh credits every month and can be cancelled anytime from your dashboard.

## 4. Manage everything in one place

Your dashboard shows your balance, plan, orders and every credit you have earned or spent. Unused credits can be refunded from the same page, within the refund window shown there.
